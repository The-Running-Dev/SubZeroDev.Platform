## Statement
A registered `IMirroredPermissionProvider` whose `LastSyncedAsync` is null, errs, or is older than `Platform:Authorization:MirrorMaximumAge` by the evaluating host's clock contributes no grant, is not asked for grants, and is recorded as `ProviderUnavailable` naming it; no configuration makes the maximum unbounded (Owner: Core.)
